FAQ

Before you install.

What is WeLookup?add

WeLookup is a Chrome extension that analyzes the public storefront open in your browser and organizes the evidence into Overview, Technology, Products, and Audit views.

Does every view work on every storefront?add

Technology detection and the general Overview can work across public storefronts. Theme evidence, Products, and Audit are shown when Shopify is confirmed and the required public data is available.

Why does WeLookup need access to the current page?add

WeLookup uses page access to run its analysis on the active public storefront after you open the extension or request a refresh. This is how it detects visible technologies and storefront signals.

Does it require a store login or admin access?add

No. WeLookup works with information exposed by the public storefront and does not require access to the merchant admin.

How does the Products scan work?add

On a confirmed Shopify storefront, you can choose a quick or full catalog scan, search the collected products and collections, and export the findings.
