FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is WeLookup?add

WeLookup is a Chrome extension that analyzes the public storefront open in your browser and organizes the findings into Overview, Technology, Products, and Audit views.

Does it require a store login or admin access?add

No. WeLookup works with information exposed by the public storefront and does not require access to the store admin.

What can it detect?add

It can identify site technologies, platforms, apps, analytics, marketing tools, and commerce services. When Shopify is confirmed, it can also surface theme evidence and Shopify-specific views.

How does the Products scan work?add

On a public Shopify storefront, you can choose a quick or full catalog scan, search the collected products and collections, and export the results.
